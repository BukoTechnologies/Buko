Hi, we’re Buko

Most small businesses aren’t breached by master hackers. They get in through a password nobody changed, an account without MFA, or an email that looked close enough to real. The fixes have existed for years — they’re just buried under dashboards built for enterprises with a full security department.

So we’re building SteelCurtain: the boring, essential 20% of security that prevents most of the trouble, set up in an afternoon and explained in plain English. No jargon, no 40-tab console, no consultant on retainer.

What SteelCurtain covers

Four things. Done properly. That’s most of your risk handled.

Multi-factor auth

Switched on for everyone, everywhere — without the week of confused support tickets that usually follows.

Password policies

Rules people can actually live with, applied across your accounts, so nobody ends up with Summer2024! again.

Phishing protection

The fakes get caught before anyone has to squint at a sender address and make a judgment call.

Alerts that matter

A message when something genuinely needs you — and blissful silence when it doesn’t.
