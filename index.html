What SteelCurtain does

Get unblocked. Stay unblocked. That’s the whole product.

Answer it honestly

Connect the accounts you already use, and we map every question to what’s actually configured. Green means yes — and you can show your work.

Fix what’s red

MFA, password rules, phishing protection — the things these forms always ask about. Switched on properly, usually inside a day.

Keep it true

Settings drift. People leave. Someone turns something off. We watch what you attested to and tell you the moment it stops being true.

Reuse it forever

The next questionnaire, the next enterprise client, next year’s insurance renewal — you’re answering from a file that’s already current.
