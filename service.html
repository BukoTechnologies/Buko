What SteelCurtain covers

No sprawling feature list. Just the things that actually move the needle for a business your size — set up properly, then watched.

Multi-factor auth

The single highest-value thing you can turn on. We roll it out across your accounts, chase the stragglers, and tell you when someone slips through the net.

Password policies

Sensible, modern rules — not the “change it every 30 days” advice that makes everyone add a number to the end. Applied once, enforced quietly.

Phishing protection

Convincing fakes get filtered before they reach an inbox, so nobody on your team has to be the last line of defence on a Friday afternoon.

Alerts that matter

Tuned to be worth reading. If SteelCurtain messages you, something actually needs a human — and we tell you what to do about it.
