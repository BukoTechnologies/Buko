Hi, we’re Buko

Buko Technologies builds software that takes something genuinely annoying and makes it feel easy. Our name comes from the coconut — tough shell, soft centre, not much fuss. That’s roughly the idea behind everything we make.

Winnow is our first product, and it exists because we do this job. We do product security for a cloud-connected machine that goes into other people’s buildings, which means we answer these questionnaires ourselves — by hand, under deal pressure, over and over.

We got tired of defending a four-hundred-row CVE list in email. So we’re building the thing we wanted.

Why we’re building this

Everything is connected now

Products that used to be inert now have a radio, a cloud backend and an update channel. Every one of those is a question your customer’s security team is entitled to ask — and a reason to stall the contract until you answer it.

The SBOM made it worse

Asking vendors for a bill of materials was the right idea. But it handed everyone a list of hundreds of vulnerabilities with no way to tell which ones matter, so both sides now argue about noise.

And now it’s the law

Regulation is arriving that expects connected products to carry a bill of materials and handle vulnerabilities properly. Soon this stops being one awkward customer and starts being a condition of selling at all.

Simplifying the world

That’s the whole brief. Winnow is where we’re starting, because the distance between what a product team can claim and what it can actually prove is about as wide as gaps get. It won’t be the last thing we build under the Buko umbrella.
